About

I'm Mehdi Seyfi, a designer and sculptor exploring the relationship between ideas, materials, and form.

My practice is driven by observation, precision, and problem-solving — transforming concepts into meaningful physical experiences. Through sculpture, product design, and handmade objects, I explore how materials such as stone, wood, metal, and bone can carry stories and create connections.

I have participated in several national and international sculpture symposiums, creating large-scale public artworks through direct engagement with raw materials and traditional making processes.

Alongside sculpture, I design and craft handmade eyewear, combining material exploration with functional design. My background in graphic design, particularly identity and logo design, has shaped my approach to creating clear, purposeful, and distinctive forms.

For me, design begins with understanding a problem and ends when an idea finds its right form.
